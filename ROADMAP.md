## Spec gap analysis

Compared with [MASTER_SPEC_SUMMARY.md](./MASTER_SPEC_SUMMARY.md):

### Done or partially in place

- **Local-first workflow and release boundary:** SQLite jobs, sequential processing, recovery, local dashboard, source/asset ledgers, automated QA, and hash-bound human approval already exist. Public publishing remains disabled.
- **Channel identity:** `channel_name` defaults to `CHANNEL_NAME_TBD`, is validated, and appears in metadata and production credits. Thumbnail artwork does not render logos or channel branding.
- **Research and script:** Wikipedia source discovery, sourced claim records, Groq/local draft generation, citation-ID validation, and human fact-review gates are implemented. Story prompts now require a fast curiosity question, geography-led explanation, claim-matched visual directions, a question/context/cause/consequence/bigger-picture/conclusion chapter arc, and explicit uncertainty. Research breadth and substantive fact verification remain human work.
- **Map engine foundation:** Natural Earth 1:110m Admin 0 Countries 5.1.1 public-domain geometry is cached with its name, pinned version, licence, source, download date, and SHA-256. A scene can zoom from a world view to a country and highlight it; generated PNG frames become hash-checked MP4 scenes in the editor. Every map visibly qualifies boundaries as Natural Earth's de-facto depiction. Explicit routes and markers require owner-supplied coordinates and recorded sources.
- **Thumbnail engine foundation:** Three differently composed 1920x1080 map-first masters are generated from Natural Earth geometry, scored on mobile readability, contrast, text size, style match, geographic selection, and title relevance, with the highest score selected and downsampled to a 1280x720 platform thumbnail. JSON recipes, a contact sheet, a 160px-wide mobile preview, and QA findings are saved. The score is a design heuristic, not a prediction of views or a substitute for human geographic review.
- **Cinematic assembly:** FFmpeg joins verified map/stock scenes and licensed stills to narration, with subtitles and optional licensed music. The video package is covered by approval hashing.
- **QA/reporting:** Critical failures block approval; map provenance, thumbnail concept count, score dimensions, contact sheet, mobile preview, and rubric thresholds are checked with actionable failure messages.

### Missing or still limited

- The map engine currently draws country polygons only. Historical-border comparisons, region boundaries, population density, migration/trade datasets, rivers, coastlines, mountains, and climate layers are not implemented. Do not invent these layers; each needs an independently sourced, versioned dataset and provenance record.
- Map-scene transitions are currently hard cuts. There is no timeline editor that schedules maps and overlays against individual narration claims, nor a geocoder or feature-selection UI.
- A live Natural Earth download and Japan map segment were smoke-tested. Automated scene tests also use deterministic fixture geometry. The map layer is Natural Earth's generalized 1:110m boundary representation, not a survey-grade or authoritative disputed-border source.
- Thumbnail geographic accuracy currently means that the requested ISO country feature exists in the pinned dataset. It does not evaluate whether the map crop, arrows, claims, or headline are contextually accurate. The three concepts are code-generated maps without optional licensed cut-out photos or source-backed labels/callouts.
- Mobile readability and contrast are heuristic checks; they do not run OCR or establish perceptual legibility across screens. Owners must inspect the 160x90 preview and contact sheet.
- A dedicated SEO/title/description/metadata agent and descriptive channel-analytics feedback remain incomplete. Current metadata is a conservative package scaffold.
- YouTube OAuth/private-first upload is not implemented. No upload or public publishing is available; implement only in a separately reviewed phase with explicit per-version approval.
- Provider-based full documentary production is not verified end-to-end. Groq, stock APIs, Edge TTS, music rights, a full 8–15-minute render, and human review each require independent validation; generated QA scores cannot replace approval.

## Known blockers and next acceptance work

- A commercially appropriate Piper voice has not been established. Use the installed eSpeak NG fallback unless voice rights are independently cleared.
- Add independent authoritative sources beyond Wikipedia and validate source facts against their citations.
- Ollama and `qwen2.5:0.5b` are installed. Inference was tested, but the model did not generate a valid-length script; the documented manual fallback stays active.
- Configure Groq, Pexels, and Pixabay credentials in ignored `config.json` before live testing Phase 5; do not commit, print, or paste keys into logs/chat.
- Edge TTS uses an online community client; confirm current Microsoft service terms before commercial use. Pixabay music must be sourced manually because the official public API does not provide music search.
- Implement stronger editorial/factual checks and human review of maps, labels, subtitles, and full-length video.
- Implement YouTube only in a separate change with OAuth consent, official API policy/quota review, private-first semantics, idempotent upload verification, and explicit release approval.
- Add migrations for future schema versions before changing the deployed schema.
- Test interruption during actual full-duration FFmpeg rendering on Windows and Linux.

The demo is intentionally not an example of real-world geography; it cannot pass approval without replacing the synthetic placeholders.
