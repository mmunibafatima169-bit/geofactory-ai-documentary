# Master specification summary

- Produce faceless, research-driven geography and country documentaries for US and international English-language viewers. Target 8–15 minutes initially, with zero recurring cost and a local-first workflow. Human approval is mandatory and tied to hashes of the exact video and metadata. YouTube upload is private-first; public publishing must never happen automatically.
- **Map Animation Engine (core feature):** provide world-to-country zooms, country and region highlights, animated borders, historical border comparisons, disputed territories with clear qualification, population density, migration and trade routes, rivers, coastlines, mountains, climate maps, animated arrows, callouts, markers and labels, and transitions between locations. Use only properly licensed datasets such as public-domain Natural Earth. Never invent borders, routes, or statistics.
- **Thumbnail Design Engine (core feature):** generate 1280×720 thumbnails with at least three genuinely different concepts per video, composed from map layers, terrain or licensed photo elements, arrows, highlighted regions and 2–5 word headlines, following the original design principles in [REFERENCE_STYLE.md](./REFERENCE_STYLE.md). Automatically score mobile readability, contrast, text size, style match, geographic accuracy, and relevance to the title; select the best; save editable sources and a JSON score record.
- **Cinematic video editor:** combine animated maps, charts, controlled camera movement, scenes tied to the narration timeline, subtitles, consistent audio levels, and optional licensed music.
- **QA:** critical failures block approval, and reports explain exactly what to improve. Scores do not guarantee views or success.
- Also include an SEO and metadata agent, an analytics agent, a persistent queue with recovery, and optional YouTube OAuth.
